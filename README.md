# Biswas Digital Hub - Professional Cyber Cafe & Online Services Website

A modern, high-converting website built for **Biswas Digital Hub**, designed specifically to engage local customers, eliminate physical queues, and drive direct WhatsApp and phone conversions for digital services.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Conversion Elements

1. **Live Service Desk Status**:
   - Real-time shop hours tracker (Mon–Sat: 8:00 AM – 9:30 PM, Sun: 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM).
   - Shows active green pulsing indicator during business hours and WhatsApp notice off-hours.

2. **Zero-Queue Remote Ordering Model**:
   - Encourages customers to send document photos/PDFs directly on WhatsApp without standing in line.
   - 100% Draft preview before final portal submission to guarantee zero rejection.

3. **Complete Service Catalog (16+ Services)**:
   - **Govt & Identity**: Aadhaar Services, PAN Card (New/Correction), Voter ID (Form 6/8).
   - **Jobs & Scholarships**: Online Govt Job Forms (SSC, Police, Railway), Swami Vivekananda (SVMCM) & Oasis Scholarships.
   - **Travel Desk**: IRCTC Confirm Train Tickets & Flights.
   - **Printing & Studio**: Crisp B/W Xerox, Color Laser Printing, High-Res Scanning, Lamination, Instant 5-Min Passport Photos.
   - **Banking & Utilities**: Money Transfer, AEPS Cash Withdrawal, Electricity Bill Payments, Mobile Recharge.
   - **Documentation**: Bengali & English Typing, Legal Stamp Paper Formatting, ATS Resumes & Bio-Data.

4. **Interactive Document Checklist Helper**:
   - Allows users to select any service (PAN Card, Voter ID, SVMCM Scholarship, Job Form, Train Ticket) to see an instant list of required documents before applying.

5. **Instant Price Estimator & Calculator**:
   - Real-time calculator where customers select services and page counts (Xerox / Color print) to get an immediate bill estimate, with a one-click button to send the order to WhatsApp.

6. **Direct Google Reviews Integration**:
   - Pre-linked to the official Google Review page: [https://g.page/r/CfvINjZcHSllEBM/review](https://g.page/r/CfvINjZcHSllEBM/review)
   - Showcases 5-star customer feedback and badges.

7. **Mobile-First Experience**:
   - Sticky bottom navigation bar for smartphone users: **Call Now**, **WhatsApp**, **Price List**, and **Google Review**.
   - Floating WhatsApp button with pulsing ping badge for desktop users.

8. **Bilingual Support**:
   - English & Bengali subtitles and quick language switch to appeal directly to local Bengali clientele.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can open the website in two ways:

### Option 1: Directly in Browser
Double-click `index.html` or drag it into any web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox).

### Option 2: Local HTTP Server (Recommended)
In terminal inside this directory, run:
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```
Then visit: `http://localhost:8080`
